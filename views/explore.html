City Park

A beautiful recreational park in Bokaro featuring a lake, greenery and peaceful surroundings, ideal for relaxation and family outings.

Best time: October to March

How to reach: Easily accessible by road from Bokaro Steel City.

Garga Dam

A scenic reservoir on the Garga River, surrounded by greenery and known for its peaceful views and natural beauty.

Best time: October to March

How to reach: About 12 km from Bokaro town; accessible by road.

Ayyappa Temple

A popular Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Ayyappa, known for its peaceful atmosphere and religious significance.

Best time: October to March

How to reach: Easily accessible by road from Bokaro Steel City.

Jagannath Temple

A prominent temple dedicated to Lord Jagannath, known for its traditional architecture and religious importance.

Best time: October to March

How to reach: Easily accessible by road from Bokaro Steel City.

Jawaharlal Nehru Park

A large zoological park featuring diverse animals and birds, along with an artificial lake, boating and a toy train.

Best time: October to March

How to reach: About 12 km from Bokaro city; accessible by road and near Bokaro Steel City railway station.

Steel Plant

One of Bokaro's major landmarks and an important industrial centre, showcasing the city's strong connection with the steel industry.

Best time: Throughout the year

How to reach: Easily accessible by road from Bokaro Steel City.
